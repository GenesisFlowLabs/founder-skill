# founder-launch

Resolve this loaded skill directory with `pwd -P`, set `SKILL_DIR` and parent `PACK_DIR`, and read `"$PACK_DIR/founder/policy.md"` plus the relevant `profiles.md` section. Use authorized `RUN`; financial details stay local.

1. Read the actual offer, evidence, delivery readiness, release constraints and current authorization. Identify exactly what can be prepared, tested, published, deployed or spent, in which environment and for which audience. Continue already authorized work without another approval loop.
2. Choose the smallest real test that answers the unresolved decision. B2B options include a documented discovery interview, a scoped demonstration of current functionality or a paid pilot with known acceptance criteria. Distinguish interest, waitlist, signed agreement, payment, deployment, usage and customer acceptance. Simulated rates cannot set a real success threshold.
3. Define the hypothesis, owner, participant criteria, evidence to collect, proposed cost/date and pass/change/stop condition. Source numerical targets from the owner's decision or known operating requirements; otherwise leave them proposed or unknown. Do not invent demand counts, fees or guaranteed savings.
4. Build a short schedule from current lead times and dependencies. Keep a user-selected date, or label an inferred date proposed. Identify the critical path, test/rollback owner and scope of customer support.
5. Write the launch run sheet and a small review cadence with actual measurement sources and decision conditions. Reuse existing release gates and trackers.

Return readiness, pilot definition, schedule, owner and one next action in the requested format; use `launch.md` only if a separate artifact is needed. Mark publication/outreach/spend that falls outside current authorization as awaiting that specific decision. Email always stays draft-only. Drafting a page is not publishing it; preparing a pilot is not running it; a successful technical check is not customer acceptance. Do not claim launch until the served/released result and required acceptance are actually verified.
