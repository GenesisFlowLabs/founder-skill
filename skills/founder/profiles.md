# Founder profiles

These are routing guides, not current customer, product or financial facts. Read the active project's brief, instructions, tracker and authorized evidence before applying them. Preserve unknown decisions.

## Genesis Flow Labs services

GFL's service decisions concern a recognizable client outcome: a workflow improvement, a delivered system or a fixed scoping result. Preserve the existing practice and offer names. Start with one buyer's workflow, cost of the current process, acceptance condition, delivery dependencies and who can authorize purchase. Separate operational user, budget owner and decision maker. Use interviews and current project records to distinguish a recurring need from one customer's custom request.

Route unclear scope to offer, price/effort feasibility to pricing plus CFO, customer acquisition to marketing, delivery repeatability to ops. Price the outcome. Record delivery effort as an internal cost assumption, never an hourly quote. Do not invent standard packages, fees, capacity, savings, testimonials or conversion rates.

## Panora Safety / Fleet

Read current Panora, Safety and Fleet source documents to establish their actual relationship, names, shipped features and proposed scope. Do not turn a module name or roadmap item into a released capability. Compare one segment and workflow at a time. Candidate purchasing roles to investigate include the operational user, safety/operations owner, budget approver and procurement or IT reviewer; these are hypotheses until sourced.

For segment selection, use real workflow evidence, buying trigger, current workaround, organization size, budget ownership, approval path, security requirements, integration dependencies and switching costs. Choose only the workflow needed for the decision: competitors for an alternatives question, offer for scope or acceptance, or direct source review/interviews for missing buyer facts. There is no required sequence. A small B2B simulated panel may expose role-specific objections after an explicit brief exists; it cannot rank real demand. Separate pilot interest, paid commitments, deployment and customer acceptance.

For subscription projections, specify the client-month or seat-month unit, period, active units and delivery/support costs. The bundled tool projects explicit monthly unit assumptions; it does not model acquisition cohorts, churn, expansion or retention. Leave those forecasts unsupported unless a separate validated model is supplied.
